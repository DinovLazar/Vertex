| `brand-mark.ts` | **Session — Brand mark (2026-08-20).** The single definition of the Vertex brand mark — the black disc carrying the VERTEX wordmark that replaced the old "V" chevron. Exports `MARK_SIZE` (1024), `MARK_BG` (`#0E0E0E`), `MARK_FG` (`#F5F5F5`), `MONOGRAM_PATH`, `WORDMARK_PATH` (composed as `MONOGRAM_PATH + WORDMARK_TAIL_PATH`, so the V exists exactly once — 743 bytes total, needs `fill-rule="evenodd"`), `MONOGRAM_CAP_RATIO` (`560/1024`), plus `brandMarkSvg({ plate, glyph, title })` and `brandMarkDataUri({ plate, glyph })`. **Two independent axes.** *Plate:* **`disc`** (transparent outside the circle — browsers, `purpose: "any"`, schema.org logo) vs **`bleed`** (full-bleed plate, no circle — iOS and Android masks, which discard alpha and impose their own silhouette). *Glyph:* **`wordmark`** (the full lockup, for 180px and up) vs **`monogram`** (the wordmark's own leading V, scaled to 55% cap height and re-centred via an SVG `transform` — for favicon surfaces, where six letters cannot resolve). 55% was picked by rendering 44/50/55/60% at 16px and 32px: it puts the stem at 1.93px on a 16px favicon while keeping the arm tips 388px from centre, inside the 512px disc. Consumed at runtime only by `src/app/opengraph-image.tsx`; everything else consumes it at build time through `scripts/build-brand-icons.ts`. **Provenance:** the mark arrived as a hard-edged 1024×1024 raster with no source vector, so `WORDMARK_PATH` is that raster's white channel traced back — pixel-boundary contours chained into loops, Douglas-Peucker at a **1.2px** tolerance. 1.2 is chosen, not arbitrary: a rasterised straight edge wanders up to ~1px off the true line, so a tolerance just above that erases the staircase artefact while leaving the R's real curvature intact.
