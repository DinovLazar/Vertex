**2026-08-20 — consulting hero: the brushed-metal plates stop squishing on phones.** `GridMotion` sized its 4 x 7 lattice as a flat `150vw x 150vh`, so every cell was a pure fraction of the viewport — fine at 1280x800 (261 x 288 per plate) but on a 375px phone the seven columns shared 562px and each plate collapsed to **67 x 293**, aspect 0.23 against desktop's 0.91. The panel treatment is meant to read as sheet metal; at 4:1 it read as vertical blinds. The box now takes a floor — `max(150vw, 1916px)` by `max(150vh, 1200px)`, derived from new `ROWS`/`COLS`/`GAP_PX`/`MIN_CELL_W`/`MIN_CELL_H` constants that also replace the `4`/`7`/`28`/`'1rem'` literals the JSX had scattered around, so the lattice and its minimum can never disagree. Above ~1277x800 the viewport term still wins and desktop is unchanged; below it the plates hold 260 x 288 and the surplus overflows the hero's three nested `overflow: hidden` frames, so a phone sees fewer, correctly-proportioned plates instead of the whole grid crushed to fit. A `max()` floor rather than a media query: no breakpoint to land wrong on foldables, landscape phones or a half-width window, and one number to keep in sync instead of two. The risk a floor could have introduced is coverage — the lattice is rotated `-15deg` and must overhang all four edges or a bare corner of `--hero-grid-bg` shows — so it was measured, not reasoned: at the floor the rotated box is 2166 x 1672 and covers L/T/R/B at 320x568, 375x812 and 844x390 with no horizontal page scroll. Parallax is unaffected (±150px of travel against ~890px of overhang, and exactly 0 on a device with no `mousemove`). One file, `GridMotion.tsx`; tsc + lint clean. Write-up: `src/_project-state/session-consulting-hero-mobile-panels_2026-08-20.md`.
